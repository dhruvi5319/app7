# Task Tracker App

## What This Is

A simple task tracker app that lets users create, manage, and track their tasks to completion. It is designed for individuals who need a lightweight way to organize their work and personal to-dos without the overhead of complex project management tools.

## Core Value

Users can quickly capture and track tasks so nothing falls through the cracks.
